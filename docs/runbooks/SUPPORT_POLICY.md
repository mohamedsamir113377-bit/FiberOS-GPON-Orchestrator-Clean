# FiberOS R14.1 — 14-Day Technical Support Policy

## Support term

The standard commercial proposal includes **14 calendar days** of technical support beginning on the support-start date stated in the signed agreement.

## Included support

The standard support scope covers:

1. one guided first deployment session using the delivered source and documented prerequisites;
2. reproducible defects in the delivered source code;
3. corrections where the delivered migration chain or buyer documentation is internally inconsistent, provided the buyer has not altered the affected source/migrations;
4. configuration guidance within the documented deployment contract;
5. reasonable guidance on reproducing and collecting logs for a source-level defect without exposing secrets or personal data.

## Not included by default

Support does not include unrestricted custom development, 24/7 monitoring, infrastructure administration, database administration, identity-provider administration, hosting, certificate management, backup restoration by the seller, on-site work, customer-data remediation, or third-party outage resolution.

Custom UI/UX, dashboards, new business workflows, and new external integrations are separately quoted engineering work.

## Third-party adapters

The delivered source may contain adapter boundaries and specific vendor implementations. A buyer request to add or materially change an external vendor integration is a separately scoped engineering engagement unless the signed agreement explicitly includes it.

## Buyer responsibilities during support

The buyer should preserve the exact delivered source, record the package version and SHA-256, avoid modifying migrations before troubleshooting, provide reproducible steps, and redact credentials/secrets from logs.

## No implied service level

Unless a signed SLA says otherwise, this policy does not promise a fixed response time, uptime, continuous monitoring, or a particular resolution time.

## Commercial/legal status

This policy describes the standard commercial offer. The signed sales agreement controls the actual support term, scope, exclusions, and remedies.
