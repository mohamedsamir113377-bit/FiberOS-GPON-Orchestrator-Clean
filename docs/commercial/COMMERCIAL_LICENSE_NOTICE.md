For online sales, the applicable license and checkout terms should be presented before payment and accepted as part of the order flow. The order record should retain the exact terms version, package version, SHA-256, license tier, purchaser, price, and timestamp.

## Rights hierarchy

1. Mandatory applicable law and third-party/open-source licenses control where applicable.
2. The versioned online checkout terms and purchased license tier define the buyer's commercial rights.
3. A separately executed agreement controls only where it expressly modifies the online commercial license and is legally valid.
